# Endgame XIAO wireless case and battery tray prototype

This is a **new case variant** for the XIAO nRF52840/150 mAh battery PCB. The original `002 CASE/TheENDGAME2024_CASE001.step` is unchanged. Rebuild the deliverables with `python tools/build_xiao_case.py` using `cadquery-ocp` 8.0.1.0.0. The exports are in `002 CASE/XIAO WIRELESS/`: separate STEP and binary STL files for the case and removable battery tray, plus a [mesh preview](../002%20CASE/XIAO%20WIRELESS/case-and-tray-preview.png). These are printable **CAD prototypes**, not a physical fit claim.

The case coordinate frame is `(PCB X − 150, 140 − PCB Y, Z)` in millimetres. The 1.6 mm PCB occupies `Z=0…1.6`; the case is above it. The keyboard's original outside span and all existing switch centres are retained. The new case cuts material where the electronic parts and cable need clearance.

The case pocket overlaps the nominal 19.75 × 26.02 × 3.8 mm [Adafruit 1317](https://www.adafruit.com/product/1317) pouch with its long axis front to back. The rounded pocket clears a **hard rectangular nominal-size proxy** at `Z=0.2…5.2`; tapes, lead tabs, and pouch swelling still need a real-pack check. The PCB opening has R1 corners, so its 22 × 28 mm bounding box is a planning envelope rather than an unobstructed sharp-cornered rectangle.

The tray spans the opening and contacts the underside of surrounding PCB laminate. The proposed pouch bottom is at `Z=0.2`, leaving 0.2 mm nominal space above the tray for an insulating/retaining layer. No case solid occupies the opening beneath `Z=0`. The tray reaches `Z=−1.0`, while the case's broad lower perimeter reaches `Z=−1.4`. Without feet, the tray alone is 0.4 mm above the case's nominal desk plane, but the screw heads extend lower.

The original [FDM case parts list](../README.md) specifies four M3 D4.6 × L3 heat-set inserts in 4 mm-deep holes, lists 6 mm countersunk and 5 mm flathead M3 screws, and explains the 5 mm board-only fit through 1.6 mm PCB. Rubber D6.6 grommets or bumpons serve as feet. Boolean probes of the original STEP confirm the **four existing insert bores** at PCB `(54,67.063)`, `(246,67.063)`, `(75.35,144.916)`, and `(224.65,144.916)`. The two central PCB holes used by this tray, `(140.195,154.169)` and `(159.805,154.170)`, have **no original case insert bore**. This variant adds two matching seats beneath those existing PCB holes, without adding PCB holes. It therefore uses **six M3 inserts and six screws total**, while the other ten grommet positions remain available for feet. The new standoffs bring the case underside to the PCB top at `Z=1.6`; the 4 mm insert bores stop at `Z=5.6`, leaving at least **1.0 mm of solid roof** and no raised exterior boss.

Use **two M3 × 6 mm DIN965 90° countersunk screws** for the tray ears, matching the countersunk length already listed in the README. The model seats a head no larger than **6.2 mm diameter × 1.65 mm height** flush with the thickened tray ears at `Z=−1.8`; the resulting threaded engagement in a seated insert is approximately **2.6 mm**, leaving 1.4 mm to the 4 mm bore bottom. Use four or more of the original rubber feet or bumpons with **at least 1.0 mm installed projection below the case's `Z=−1.4` support plane**. This sets the desk plane to at most `Z=−2.4` and leaves **at least 0.6 mm nominal clearance** to the recessed screw heads and ear bottoms. The D6.6 grommet diameter alone does not establish projection; choose or supplement feet to achieve the stated installed height. Check screw seating and avoid overdriving the battery-adjacent inserts.

## Assembly path and access

The electrical design keeps direct battery-wire landing J1 at PCB `(148,147.5)`/`(152,147.5)` and two strain-relief holes at `(148,150.5)`/`(152,150.5)`. Place the protected pouch in the PCB opening, lead exit toward `Y=144`, and route insulated leads in the case channel toward J1. Solder and insulate the leads, secure them through the strain-relief holes, then install the lower tray with M3 × 6 screws through the two existing central board holes into the added heat-set inserts. The centre between the tray ears is clear for the power switch. Removing those screws releases the tray for battery service.

The XIAO's BAT+/GND pads use **two separate short insulated jumpers** from underside board lands near PCB `(146,89)`/`(149,89)` up through the board's access slot near `X=143.7…147.4`, `Y=70.7…74.85`. These wires run on the PCB underside (`Z≤0`), away from this case's F.Cu-side pocket; cover exposed conductor with polyimide insulation and restrain the jumpers to the PCB before case assembly. No long wire tunnel from the pouch to the XIAO is needed. The XIAO case cavity leaves the antenna end of the module beneath nonmetal case material; keep the host copper keepout and avoid metal-filled case material in that region.

## Geometry checks and unresolved fit

The generator verifies that both exported parts are valid single solids, that the case and tray do not overlap, that the nominal pouch, XIAO, USB, U3, and power-switch proxy volumes do not intersect the modified case, and that **at least 1.25 mm of case material** remains above the full enlarged rectangular battery pocket from `Z=5.35` to `Z=6.60`. It also checks the two new heat-set bores, their 1.0 mm local roof, countersunk screw-head seating, and the stated foot clearance. Reimporting the STEP files and checking the binary STLs confirmed valid solids and watertight meshes. All **36 conservative 15 × 15 mm rotated switch-body volumes** have zero intersection with the revised case; the nearest separation remains approximately **1.60 mm**. The tray stays below the PCB and has approximately **4 mm minimum plan clearance** to those switch-body footprints (nearest SW19/SW23), though the assembled hot-swap sockets still need a physical underside check.

The XIAO's 17.8 × 21 mm board outline and the USB connector's approximate **9 × 7.35 mm planar footprint** come from Seeed's [published mechanical resources](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/XIAO_BLE/) and [v1.2 KiCad board](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/XIAO-BLE/Res/260828_Seeed_Studio_XIAO_nRF52840_v1.2.zip). **Assembled Z heights of the module, USB shell, and cable plug were not verified from a vendor drawing.** The `Z=6.4` module cavity and `Z=7.2` USB passage are prototype allowances, not validated manufacturer clearances. A particular USB plug may need a wider mouth. The recessed power actuator may need a fitted slider extension for finger access. Verify these features with the actual module, plug, switch, assembled PCB, printed case, screws, and battery before calling this a working enclosure.
