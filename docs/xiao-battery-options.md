# Thin protected LiPo options for XIAO nRF52840

**Recommendation: Adafruit 1317, 150 mAh, remains the practical one-off candidate in Canada, subject to confirming current stock.** PiShop.ca lists it at CA$7.95, and Adafruit publishes the complete pack dimensions and a 127.5 mm lead. Its supplied JST-PH plug will not be used: remove it and solder the leads directly to the PCB's labeled battery pads. Budget around **4.5–5 mm** for the pack area to allow room above its listed 3.8 mm thickness for dimensional variation, surface tape, and avoiding compression; the vendor does not publish a dimensional tolerance. Provide a wire channel and strain relief so the lead exit is not pinched or pulled.

If every fraction of a millimetre matters and a custom bare-wire connection is acceptable, LiPol LP302030 is thinner: **3.0 ±0.3 mm** overall including its protection circuit. Its official order flow is sample/contact based and lists **US$99 economy / US$199 express shipping to Canada**, so it is a poor one-off purchase. No readily purchasable Canadian stock listing surfaced in this check.

## XIAO charging and integration notes

Seeed’s [XIAO nRF52840 wiki](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/XIAO_BLE/#battery-charging-current) documents selectable charging current of about **50 mA or 100 mA** during the constant-current phase; current tapers near full charge. For these small cells, use the low (~50 mA) setting. The Adafruit cell’s manufacturer datasheet gives 150 mAh nominal and 142 mAh minimum capacity, so the initial charge rate is about 0.33C nominal (0.35C at minimum capacity). LiPol’s 130 mAh minimum capacity gives about 0.38C, under its 0.5C standard-charge rating.

For direct soldering, Adafruit's JST-PH plug is unused: remove it by cutting one conductor at a time, insulating each cut end, then solder the positive and negative leads to the PCB's labeled BAT+ and BAT− pads after confirming polarity. Do not let the stripped leads touch each other. Add strain relief and route the wires through a channel with enough slack that case assembly cannot pinch or tug them. The protection circuit is useful fault protection, but it is not a substitute for the board's correctly configured single-cell CC/CV charger. Avoid physically squeezing or puncturing the pouch.
