# Endgame nice!nano / 401730 case prototype

The revised case and removable battery tray are generated from the original Endgame STEP with [`tools/build_nice_nano_case.py`](../tools/build_nice_nano_case.py). Run it with `/tmp/endgame-fit-env/bin/python tools/build_nice_nano_case.py` or another Python environment containing OCP, trimesh, Matplotlib, and NumPy. The original STEP and the separate XIAO variant are unchanged. The new [case STEP](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/TheENDGAME2024_NICE_NANO_CASE.step), [case STL](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/TheENDGAME2024_NICE_NANO_CASE.stl), [tray STEP](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/TheENDGAME2024_NICE_NANO_BATTERY_TRAY.step), [tray STL](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/TheENDGAME2024_NICE_NANO_BATTERY_TRAY.stl), and [mesh preview](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/case-and-tray-preview.png) are a digital prototype. This fit revision has not been printed or assembled; it responds to rubbing and unwanted features reported on the earlier print.

Coordinates below use `(PCB X − 150, 140 − PCB Y, Z)` in millimetres. The 1.6 mm PCB occupies `Z=0…1.6`; the case sits above it. The original sloped exterior and overall height are retained. Deeper internal pockets make room for the battery and controller. The controller hood and side shoulders have been removed after a full-height cap-envelope check revealed intrusion into adjacent Chicago Stenographer keycap envelopes. The case is widened by 4 mm: the original sections outside case X=±12 mm move outward 2 mm, and matching sections bridge the two resulting gaps. All 36 keys and the outer mounting features move with their halves; the controller, LED, battery opening, central tray mounts, J1 wire landing, and SW38 power-switch access stay fixed. The original keycap-to-outer-edge borders are preserved. The latest fit revision restores the complete original roof surface around both inner thumbs and retains the USB relief; see the [thumb repair comparison](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/thumb-roof-repair-preview.png) and [USB sections](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/case-fit-refinements.png).

**Tray revision, 2026-10-16:** the relief responds to the user-reported need to cut a notch at the end opposite the screws so the tray sits flat over the resistor solder joints. R2 pad centres are at case `(2.07,32.40)` and `(12.23,32.40)`; their 1.6 mm copper pads overlap the old tray end/corner. Both reliefs pass through the tray's full thickness and open out of its free end. All four R1/R2 joint proxies (3.6 × 3.6 mm, extending 2 mm below the PCB) clear the revised tray; the minimum proxy-to-tray gap is 0.43 mm. The screw region and case geometry are unchanged. These proxies allow 1 mm beyond the copper in each direction; actual hand-soldered fillets and the new print remain unmeasured. See the [tray comparison](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/battery-tray-solder-clearance.png) and [clearance report](validation/nice-nano-case/tray-solder-clearance.json).

**Controller height is an assembly limit.** The case assumes the *entire* nice!nano assembly, including the USB shell and underside projections, fits within `Z=2.6…6.0`. Relative to the host PCB top at `Z=1.6`, this requires the lowest module projection to clear the PCB by at least 1.0 mm and every assembled feature to remain at or below `Z=6.0`. Use short individual soldered posts with a height-setting jig or spacer and measure the completed assembly before enclosure fitting. Standard low-profile sockets have not been qualified for this case: their published above-board height refers to the module-board plane, while the stated module thickness includes projections on both sides, so the two figures cannot simply be added. Any socket installation must independently meet both complete-envelope limits. The actual module revision, posts, USB shell, solder joints, and cable plug were not measured here. The host PCB should keep copper clear beneath the radio antenna.

The generator verifies valid single solids, no case/tray overlap, component proxy clearance, intact battery and LED roofs, the two new insert bores and screw seats, and no collision with the 36 rotated 15 × 15 mm switch-body envelopes. Minimum switch-body clearance is **1.6 mm**. Reimported STEP parts remain valid single solids; both exported STL meshes are watertight and consistently wound. The [geometry report](validation/nice-nano-case/geometry.json) records the mesh volumes and envelope limits.

The earlier static 17 × 17 mm keycap proxy at PCB-bottom `Z=8…13` did not test press clearance: it missed that the controller hood intruded into adjacent keycap space. After removing the hood and restoring the stock roof/opening contours, a full-height sweep of nominal 1U Chicago Stenographer envelopes (17.5 × 16.5 mm, centered on each switch after the 2 mm outward half-shift and rotated with it) from PCB-top `Z=1.6` through `Z=20` clears all 36 locations. Minimum gap is **0.70 mm**, equal to the original case's own minimum on the original PCB; every key keeps at least its original-case clearance, including SW16 and SW20 beside the restored alpha edges. Both supplied sheet-plate STEP files and both printed-plate STEP files also have zero intersection with the revised case; the added fills remain at least 0.2 mm above the plates. The original 227 mm-wide case does not fit the shifted key field; use the new 231 mm-wide case with this PCB. See the [CS clearance preview](validation/nice-nano-case/cs-clearance.png) and [clearance data](validation/nice-nano-case/cs-clearance.json). This is a nominal rectangular-envelope CAD check, not physical print proof or validation of larger thumb caps and sculpted skirts. Choc V2 requires the MX-stem CS variant; the linked [Asymplex CS Choc page](https://www.asymplex.xyz/product/cs-chicago-stenographer-profile) describes Choc V1 stems. Verify actual keycap travel, hot-swap sockets, installed controller height, USB plug, pack, screw seating, and PLA print tolerances on a physical prototype before manufacturing or use.

The [centreline side profile](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/case-side-profile.png) overlays the revised section with the original case. Both have the same maximum Z; the original top slopes from roughly 6.6 mm at the front to 10.13 mm at the back. All quoted Z values use the PCB-bottom datum, not thickness measured normal to that sloping surface.

## Choc V2 switch plates

Optional plates hold Choc V2 switches more securely in this case. They are the same design as the [original-case plates](choc-v1-v2.md#choc-v2-switch-plates), regenerated for this case and the 2 mm outward key shift. Files: [`001 PCB/PLATE CHOC V2/NICE NANO WIRELESS/`](../001%20PCB/PLATE%20CHOC%20V2/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/) ([preview](../001%20PCB/PLATE%20CHOC%20V2/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/plate_preview.png)). The plates made for the original case do not fit here.

- **3-D printed (recommended):** `3D PRINT/`. 2.2 mm thick, resting on the PCB top, with a 1.2 mm clip lip, 14.1 mm cutouts and 0.4 mm wall clearance. Print top face down, without supports. The outer-thumb test piece has the same shape as the original-case test piece, so a fit already confirmed with that piece carries over.
- **1.2 mm sheet (FR-4 or metal, untested):** JLCPCB Gerber ZIPs, DXF, STEP and STL; 14.0 mm cutouts and 0.3 mm wall clearance.

Each side has three pieces, and left and right are mirror images. Unlike the original case, the alpha well here opens into the central controller cavity at plate height. The alpha pieces' inner edge is therefore not set by a wall. It runs 2 mm beyond the inner column's 15 mm switch housings, at case X = ±10.2 mm, about 1.3 mm outside the nice!nano. The plate is located by the outer walls and the switches.

Every piece also stays at least the wall clearance away from the top-side parts' courtyards: U2 (including the USB end), LED1, C1, R1, R2, J1 and SW38. A 3-D check against this case STEP shows zero intersection. The narrowest web is 1.65 mm (sheet plate). See `plate-report.json` and `3D PRINT/print-plate-report.json`.

Regenerate with:

```sh
KPY=/Applications/KiCad/KiCad.app/Contents/Frameworks/Python.framework/Versions/Current/bin/python3
$KPY tools/export_switches.py /tmp/nn-switches.json \
    --board "001 PCB/KICAD/NICE NANO WIRELESS/TheEndgame2024_NiceNano.kicad_pcb" --keepouts /tmp/nn-keepouts.json
CASE="$PWD/002 CASE/NICE NANO WIRELESS/TheENDGAME2024_NICE_NANO_CASE.step"
cd tools && python3 make_choc_v2_print_plate.py --switches /tmp/nn-switches.json --keepouts /tmp/nn-keepouts.json --case "$CASE" --output /tmp/nn-print-plate
python3 make_choc_v2_plate.py --switches /tmp/nn-switches.json --keepouts /tmp/nn-keepouts.json --case "$CASE" --output /tmp/nn-plate
cd .. && $KPY tools/export_plate_gerbers.py /tmp/nn-plate
```

## Optional clear LED logo (two-filament print)

[`tools/build_nice_nano_led_logo.py`](../tools/build_nice_nano_led_logo.py) splits the published case into an opaque body and a transparent logo inlay over the status LED. The logo comes from [`runty.svg`](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/LED%20LOGO/runty.svg). It is 14 × 11.3 mm and centred on the LED at case `(0, 36.5)`; pass `--width` to resize it. The inlay is a full-depth column from the LED pocket ceiling (`Z=3.55`) to the roof (≈8.6 mm), so light passes only through clear filament. The two parts add up exactly to the standard case. Where the column crosses the battery or R1 pocket, it forms that pocket's ceiling. The narrowest band is 1.3 mm. About 11 mm² of the 25 mm² LED face sits directly under clear material; the outer arcs are lit only by light travelling sideways through the inlay, so they will be dimmer.

For a Bambu dual-filament print, open [`TheENDGAME2024_NICE_NANO_CASE_LED_LOGO.3mf`](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/LED%20LOGO/TheENDGAME2024_NICE_NANO_CASE_LED_LOGO.3mf). It loads as one object with two parts: `case body (opaque)` and `LED logo (transparent)`. Assign the transparent filament to the logo part. The separate `_BODY` and `_CLEAR` STL/STEP files are also provided; if importing the STLs, load them together as one object with multiple parts. See the [preview](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/LED%20LOGO/led-logo-preview.png) and [report](validation/nice-nano-case/led-logo.json). This has not been printed.
