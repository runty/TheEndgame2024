# Endgame nice!nano / 401730 case prototype

The revised case and removable battery tray are generated from the original Endgame STEP with [`tools/build_nice_nano_case.py`](../tools/build_nice_nano_case.py). Run it with `/tmp/endgame-fit-env/bin/python tools/build_nice_nano_case.py` or another Python environment containing OCP, trimesh, Matplotlib, and NumPy. The original STEP and the separate XIAO variant are unchanged. The new [case STEP](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/TheENDGAME2024_NICE_NANO_CASE.step), [case STL](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/TheENDGAME2024_NICE_NANO_CASE.stl), [tray STEP](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/TheENDGAME2024_NICE_NANO_BATTERY_TRAY.step), [tray STL](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/TheENDGAME2024_NICE_NANO_BATTERY_TRAY.stl), and [mesh preview](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/case-and-tray-preview.png) are a digital prototype. They have not been printed or assembled.

Coordinates below use `(PCB X − 150, 140 − PCB Y, Z)` in millimetres. The 1.6 mm PCB occupies `Z=0…1.6`; the case sits above it. The original sloped exterior and overall height are retained. Deeper internal pockets make room for the battery and controller. The controller hood and side shoulders have been removed after a full-height cap-envelope check revealed intrusion into adjacent Chicago Stenographer keycap envelopes. The source case perimeter, all 36 switch positions, existing mounting features, J1 direct-wire pad/strain-relief location, and SW38 power-switch access are retained.

**Controller height is an assembly limit.** The case assumes the *entire* nice!nano assembly, including the USB shell and underside projections, fits within `Z=2.6…6.0`. Relative to the host PCB top at `Z=1.6`, this requires the lowest module projection to clear the PCB by at least 1.0 mm and every assembled feature to remain at or below `Z=6.0`. Use short individual soldered posts with a height-setting jig or spacer and measure the completed assembly before enclosure fitting. Standard low-profile sockets have not been qualified for this case: their published above-board height refers to the module-board plane, while the stated module thickness includes projections on both sides, so the two figures cannot simply be added. Any socket installation must independently meet both complete-envelope limits. The actual module revision, posts, USB shell, solder joints, and cable plug were not measured here. The host PCB should keep copper clear beneath the radio antenna.

The generator verifies valid single solids, no case/tray overlap, component proxy clearance, intact battery and LED roofs, the two new insert bores and screw seats, and no collision with the 36 rotated 15 × 15 mm switch-body envelopes. Minimum switch-body clearance is **1.6 mm**. Reimported STEP parts remain valid single solids; both exported STL meshes are watertight and consistently wound. The [geometry report](validation/nice-nano-case/geometry.json) records the mesh volumes and envelope limits.

The earlier static 17 × 17 mm keycap proxy at PCB-bottom `Z=8…13` did not test press clearance: it missed that the controller hood intruded into adjacent keycap space. After removing the hood and restoring the stock roof/opening contours, a full-height sweep of nominal 1U Chicago Stenographer envelopes (17.5 × 16.5 mm, centered on each original switch location and rotated with it) from PCB-top `Z=1.6` through `Z=20` clears all 36 locations. Minimum gap is **0.70 mm**, including both circled positions, SW16 and SW20. The original case gives the same reported clearances. See the [CS clearance preview](validation/nice-nano-case/cs-clearance.png) and [clearance data](validation/nice-nano-case/cs-clearance.json). This is a nominal rectangular-envelope CAD check, not physical print proof or validation of larger thumb caps and sculpted skirts. Choc V2 requires the MX-stem CS variant; the linked [Asymplex CS Choc page](https://www.asymplex.xyz/product/cs-chicago-stenographer-profile) describes Choc V1 stems. Verify actual keycap travel, hot-swap sockets, installed controller height, USB plug, pack, screw seating, and PLA print tolerances on a physical prototype before manufacturing or use.

The [centreline side profile](../002%20CASE/NICE%20NANO%20WIRELESS/case-side-profile.png) overlays the revised section with the original case. Both have the same maximum Z; the original top slopes from roughly 6.6 mm at the front to 10.13 mm at the back. All quoted Z values use the PCB-bottom datum, not thickness measured normal to that sloping surface.
